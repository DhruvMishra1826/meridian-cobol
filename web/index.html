Precision first.

Monetary values use integer minor units: 12500 represents 125.00 in your chosen currency. This workspace does not convert currencies.

Dates & identifiers

Enter dates as YYYYMMDD. IDs use uppercase letters, digits, hyphens and underscores. Names and memos cannot contain |, ~ or control characters.

Invoice workflow

  1. Create a customer and a draft invoice.
  2. Add stock items as invoice lines.
  3. Issue the invoice to post receivables, revenue, tax and cost of goods.
  4. Record payment against the open balance.

Reversals & closing

Reverse an entry with a fresh reference. Closing a period rejects postings dated on or before the closed-through date.
